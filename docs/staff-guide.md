# Staff guide: the chat assistant's staff page

This guide is for school staff who answer admission enquiries and parents' concerns.
You only need a web browser.

---

## 1. Log in

1. Open the staff page. The address is given to you by the person who runs the chat assistant.
   It ends with **/staff** (for example `https://chat.yourschool.in/staff`).
2. Type the staff password. Press **Log in**.
3. You stay logged in for 8 hours. To leave, press **Log out** (top right).

**If it does not work**
- "Wrong password": check the password and try again.
- "Too many wrong attempts": wait 15 minutes, then try again.
- "Staff login is switched off": tell the person who runs the chat assistant.

**Keep the password secret.** Do not write it on paper near the computer. Do not send it in WhatsApp.

---

## 2. Read new admission enquiries

1. Look at the box **Admission enquiries**. The newest enquiry is at the top.
2. Each row shows: the reference (like **E-1001**), the parent, the child and class, the mobile number,
   when they want to visit, who it was sent to, the status and the date.
3. Press **Refresh** (top right) to see new enquiries.

You also get an email for each new enquiry.
If an enquiry stays **New** for 1 working day, the counsellor gets a reminder.
After 2 working days, the front office gets an email too.

---

## 3. Change a status

Every enquiry, concern and callback has a **Status** box. Click it and choose the new status.
It saves at once. A small message says it was saved.

**Admission enquiries**

| Status | Use it when |
|---|---|
| New | Nobody has called the parent yet |
| Contacted | You called or emailed the parent |
| Visit booked | The parent will visit the school on a date you agreed |
| Closed | Finished (joined, or not interested) |

**Parent concerns**

| Status | Use it when |
|---|---|
| Received | Nobody has looked at it yet |
| In progress | You are working on it |
| Resolved | It is solved |

**Callback requests**: **New** until you call the person, then **Called back**.

Please change the status every time. Parents can see the status of their concern in the chat.
Reminders stop when you change the status.

---

## 4. Answer a concern

1. Look at the box **Parent concerns (tickets)**.
   A red **Urgent** label means safety, health or bullying.
2. **Urgent concerns: act now.** Call the parent at once. Tell the principal.
3. Other concerns:
   1. Read the concern.
   2. Change the status to **In progress**. Do this within 1 working day, or a reminder email is sent.
   3. Call or email the parent with the mobile number in the row. Say the reference (like **T-1001**).
   4. When it is solved, change the status to **Resolved**.
4. If it is not resolved in 3 working days, the backup person (for example the principal) gets an email.

Do not say "complaint" to the parent. Say "concern" or "suggestion".

---

## 5. Delete a person's data (when they ask)

A parent may ask the school to delete their details. Do this the same day.

1. Go to the box **Delete a person's data (on request)** (near the bottom of the page).
2. Type the parent's mobile number. Press **Find**.
3. Read the message. It says what is stored, for example "1 enquiry, 2 concerns".
   If it says something is **still open**, finish or note it first if needed. Deleting removes it too.
4. Type **DELETE** (in capital letters) in the box.
5. Press **Delete everything for this number**.
6. Check the **Audit log** below. A new line says what was deleted. (It never shows the number.)

This cannot be undone. Tell the parent it is done.

---

## 6. Other parts of the page

- **Questions the bot could not answer**: questions people asked that the school's information does not cover yet.
  Send them to the person who updates the chat content. When the answer is added, press **Content added**.
- **Outbox**: every email the assistant sent. "failed" means the email did not go. Press **Resend**, or tell the
  person who runs the chat assistant.
- **Export CSV**: downloads a table for Excel. Keep these files private and delete them when you are done.
- **Red banner "Possible attack on the staff login"**: someone is trying many passwords. You can still log in.
  Tell the person who runs the chat assistant.
- **Yellow banner "Demo data"**: the page shows test data, not real parents. Do not call these numbers.

---

## 7. Privacy rules

- Only use parents' details to answer their request.
- Do not copy numbers or names into WhatsApp groups, personal phones or other files.
- Do not share screenshots of this page.
- Log out when you leave the computer.
