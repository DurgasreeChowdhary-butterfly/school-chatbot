## Process
1. Enquiry: by phone, walk-in or the website chat.
2. Campus visit and counselling with the admission counsellor (in person only).
3. Admission test for Grade 2 and above. Pre-primary to Grade 1 have an informal interaction instead.
4. Application form submitted with documents.
5. Confirmation: pay the admission confirmation amount.
6. Submit remaining documents. Admission is final after document verification.

## Age criteria (as on 31 May of the admission year)
- Nursery: 3 years. LKG: 4 years. UKG: 5 years. Grade 1: 6 years.
- A child below the minimum age may be considered only with a written request from the parent and approval of the principal. Do not promise this.

## Classes offered
Nursery to Grade 10.

## Admission test
Held on weekends in March and April. Dates are announced by the admission office.
