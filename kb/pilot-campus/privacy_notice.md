# Privacy notice for the chat
> DUMMY DATA. Fictional school, made up for development. Replace this file with the school's approved privacy notice before any real use.

- When you leave an enquiry, share a concern or ask for a callback, we collect only what is needed: your name, your mobile number, what you tell us and, for admission enquiries, your child's name and class.
- We use these details only to answer this request. Only the school staff who handle it can see them (for example the admission counsellor, the front office or the staff member responsible for your concern).
- The chat assistant uses an AI service to understand your messages. Please do not share Aadhaar numbers, bank details or health records in the chat.
- Records are kept for 12 months and then deleted. To see or delete your details, contact the front office.
