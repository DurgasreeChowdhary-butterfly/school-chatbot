Q: How is your school different from others?
A: Small classes of at most 25 in primary, value-based education and the Little Explorers and Foundation Track programmes.

Q: Can I visit the campus before applying?
A: Yes. Campus visits and counselling are in person. Please ask for a time with the admission counsellor.

Q: Do you offer hostel?
A: No, hostel is not available at this campus.

Q: What is the school's timing?
A: Classes run from 8:45 to 15:15 for Grade 1 and above. Pre-primary ends at 12:30.

Q: When are ID cards, uniforms and books given?
A: Within the first month of the academic year. For pending items, contact the front office.
